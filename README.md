Developmental Artificial Mind

Private research project Copyright (c) Riccardo Bronzuoli · 2026

Overview

Developmental Artificial Mind is a long-term research project focused on the construction of an artificial cognitive system that develops over time, rather than being assembled as a finished set of capabilities.
The core premise is simple but strict: the system is born with a complete structural architecture but empty of knowledge. Development is treated as a continuous transformation of existing capacities, not as the discrete activation of modules.
The project deliberately rejects the common pattern of “installing” higher cognitive functions when certain thresholds are reached. Instead, every capacity exists from the beginning in a partial form and matures gradually.
This is not an attempt to reproduce the human mind, nor a large language model trained on human data. It is an exploration of developmental principles applied to artificial agents, with strong emphasis on methodological rigor.
Current Status (October 2026)

The project is in an advanced foundation phase.
What has been established and internally validated:
* A deterministic core with strict control over randomness, time, and identity
* A complete system of contracts and interfaces
* Extensive automated verification infrastructure (static analysis, tests, traceability, and constitutional gates)
* A formal separation between what is documented, implemented, tested, and actually validated
* A structured experimental framework in which prototypes are always accompanied by a working baseline and pre-declared failure criteria
* Significant internal documentation and audit trails
What is still under active development:
* Integration and maturation of the cognitive components
* Longitudinal developmental experiments (comparisons between early and later stages)
* External validation and reproducibility outside the primary development environment
The project maintains a high internal bar: a claim is considered valid only when it is supported by observable evidence, not by documentation or by the mere existence of code.
Guiding Principles (public summary)

A small number of non-negotiable rules shape the entire effort:
1. Development is continuous, never a switch.
2. No hidden global state. All sources of non-determinism must be explicit and injected.
3. Experimental prototypes are never allowed to become the sole implementation of a capability.
4. “Documented”, “implemented”, “tested”, and “validated” are four distinct states and must never be confused.
These principles are enforced by automated checks and by the overall development process.
